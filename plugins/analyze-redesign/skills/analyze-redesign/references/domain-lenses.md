# Domain Lenses

Apply only the lenses relevant to the current product. Domain guidance changes priorities; it does not replace direct evidence, platform rules, accessibility, or jurisdiction-specific requirements.

For deep work on games, use `game-experience.md` as the primary domain module. For deep enterprise, admin, dashboard, marketplace, data, or AI work, use `enterprise-data-ai.md`. The summaries below remain routing and cross-domain checks, not substitutes for those dedicated modules.

## Marketing and growth

- Test audience-message fit, value proposition, proof, differentiation, objections, pricing comprehension, and CTA continuity.
- Map acquisition source to landing promise, product reality, activation, retention, and cancellation; do not optimize the landing page in isolation.
- Distinguish qualified conversion from accidental clicks, coercion, low-quality leads, or future support burden.
- Check consent, sponsorship, testimonials, comparative claims, scarcity, recurring terms, and attribution for transparency.
- Preserve fast loading, semantic structure, accessibility, and search discoverability when in scope.

## Ecommerce and checkout

- Trace product discovery, comparison, variant/stock clarity, cart, checkout, payment, confirmation, fulfillment, returns, refund, and support.
- Make total cost, delivery timing, taxes/fees, recurring terms, inventory, restrictions, and return conditions visible before commitment.
- Support guest checkout where the operating model allows; delay account creation or optional data collection.
- Preserve entered data, support autofill and paste, explain payment failures, prevent duplicate orders, and make retry safety clear.
- Test promotions, address formats, currency, locale, unavailable items, split shipment, partial refund, and mobile keyboard behavior.

## Marketplace and multi-sided platforms

- Map each side's incentives, onboarding, identity, inventory/supply, search or matching, messaging, transaction, dispute, reputation, and retention.
- Verify status, obligations, visibility, and fees from every role; one side's “simple” step may create hidden labor for another.
- Check ranking transparency, sponsorship, cold start, fraud, off-platform pressure, cancellations, no-shows, proof, escrow, moderation, and appeals.
- Design trust without reducing people to a single rating; explain review provenance and prevent retaliation where applicable.
- Model liquidity and network effects as product constraints, not excuses for manipulative defaults.

## Enterprise, workflow, admin, and SaaS

- Map roles, permissions, objects, lifecycle states, approvals, exceptions, audit history, ownership, handoffs, and result destinations.
- Separate monitoring, analysis, action, configuration, and administration rather than mixing them in one surface.
- Support expert efficiency with saved views, bulk actions, shortcuts, command patterns, templates, and predictable density without harming learnability.
- Make scope, tenant/workspace, environment, data provenance, freshness, unsaved state, and permission consequences visible.
- Test long-running jobs, concurrent edits, drafts, versioning, imports/exports, partial success, rollback, session expiry, and support escalation.

## Dashboards, analytics, and monitoring

- Start with decisions and questions, then select metrics and charts; a dashboard is not a collection of available data.
- Show definition, unit, time zone, window, comparison basis, filters, source, freshness, uncertainty, missing data, and last update.
- Use chart type, scale, sorting, aggregation, color, annotation, and interaction that preserve truthful comparison.
- Avoid truncated axes or dual axes that distort; disclose transformations, forecasts, estimates, and suppressed values.
- Provide accessible labels, summaries, tabular alternatives, keyboard interaction, non-color encoding, and export where needed.
- For monitoring, separate healthy, warning, incident, acknowledged, and resolved states; define ownership and next action.

## Financial, health, identity, and regulated flows

- Identify jurisdiction, risk classification, required disclosures, record retention, consent, accessibility, and human-review obligations before redesign.
- Make amounts, rates, dates, uncertainty, eligibility, evidence, authorization, and irreversible consequences explicit.
- Design review, correction, confirmation, receipt, audit trail, fraud protection, dispute, appeal, and recovery proportional to harm.
- Minimize sensitive data and exposure in notifications, shared devices, logs, analytics, screenshots, and support tools.
- Never present heuristic review as legal, medical, financial, security, or compliance approval; route decisions to qualified review.

## Government and public services

- Map the whole service across eligibility, evidence, application, casework, decision, notification, payment, support, appeal, and renewal.
- Assume stress, low literacy, intermittent access, shared devices, language variation, disability, and assisted-digital channels.
- Explain why information is requested, acceptable evidence, save/resume, expected timing, status, missing items, and escalation.
- Treat denial, deadline, identity mismatch, bereavement, safeguarding, and appeal as first-class journeys.
- Prefer plain language and researched patterns, while verifying jurisdiction-specific law and policy.

## AI and automated systems

- State the user's goal, the model's role, data boundaries, expected capability, uncertainty, latency, cost, and failure consequences.
- Set expectations before interaction; explain source/provenance, confidence, limitations, and material changes at the moment they matter.
- Support inspect, edit, regenerate, compare, constrain, undo, override, escalate, and provide feedback without forcing automation.
- Design for hallucination, unsafe output, bias, prompt injection, data leakage, automation surprise, drift, and conflicting human/model authority.
- Evaluate task quality and harm with representative data and human judgment; engagement alone is not AI quality.
- Distinguish generated, retrieved, inferred, recommended, and human-authored content.

## Games and interactive experiences

- Evaluate first-run comprehension, core loop, controls, feedback, challenge, progression, reward, pause/resume, settings, save, failure, recovery, and exit.
- Check remapping, input alternatives, target timing, difficulty options, camera, motion, flashes, color, text, subtitles, audio cues, and cognitive load.
- Preserve response feel and frame stability; test touch targets, orientation, platform back, interruptions, controller disconnect, and cloud/local save conflict.
- Separate exact gameplay evidence from concept art, trailers, or promotional scenes.
- Inspect monetization, chance, scarcity, streaks, parental controls, social pressure, reporting, and age-appropriate safeguards.

## Learning and guided experiences

- Align learning objective, prerequisites, instruction, example, practice, feedback, assessment, retention, and transfer.
- Use retrieval and spaced practice where appropriate; do not confuse completion, time spent, or animation with learning.
- Keep onboarding contextual, skippable, resumable, and discoverable later.
- Explain why an answer is wrong and how to improve; support retry without exposing the answer prematurely.
- Synchronize narration, captions, visuals, transcript, controls, speed, progress, and resume state.

## Social, community, and creator products

- Map creation, audience, discovery, feedback, moderation, reputation, collaboration, monetization, ownership, export, and deletion.
- Make audience and persistence visible before posting; protect drafts and support correction.
- Design block, mute, report, appeal, sensitive-content, anti-harassment, rate-limit, and crisis/escalation flows.
- Avoid engagement ranking that hides user control or amplifies foreseeable harm; expose meaningful preference controls where feasible.
- Clarify rights, attribution, reuse, sponsorship, AI generation, and revenue terms.

## Developer tools and technical products

- Respect expert workflows: keyboard, copy/paste, selection, search, diff, history, undo, reproducibility, automation, and deep links.
- Show environment, scope, permissions, version, command impact, logs, progress, partial failure, and recovery.
- Keep code, identifiers, paths, output, errors, and structured data copyable and bidi-safe.
- Provide progressive disclosure without hiding exact configuration or diagnostics.
- Test empty/large repositories, slow tasks, conflicts, offline state, session expiry, and destructive commands.

## Content, media, and publishing

- Separate discovery, evaluation, reading/viewing, saving, sharing, subscription, accessibility, and return journeys.
- Design information scent, metadata, recency, authorship, corrections, related content, and trustworthy recommendations.
- Protect reading rhythm, line length, hierarchy, media controls, captions/transcripts, and distraction management.
- Make paywalls, trials, renewal, cancellation, ads, sponsorship, personalization, and tracking transparent.
- Support structured content and templates without erasing editorial exceptions or multilingual needs.

## Mobile, responsive, Persian, and RTL

- Recompose hierarchy and navigation at breakpoints; do not merely compress desktop UI.
- Test safe areas, virtual keyboard, scroll restoration, sticky regions, overlays, gestures, orientation, platform back, and interruption.
- For Persian or bilingual products, activate the full platform/localization module and verify bidi, mixed scripts, numerals, dates, punctuation, icons, tables, charts, fields, search normalization, and truncation.
- Choose typography with tested Persian glyph quality and required weights. Treat translation, localization, and content adaptation as different tasks.
- Verify that semantic reading order, focus order, visual progression, and motion remain correct in RTL and LTR.
